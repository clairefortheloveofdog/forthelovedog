FAQ & Policies

Everything you need to know about training, booking, and working together.

Welcome to For the Love of Dog

My goal is to provide supportive, positive, relationship-focused training that helps you and your dog learn and grow together.

There is no such thing as a perfect dog or perfect dog guardian. Training takes time, patience, consistency, and understanding. I'm here to help guide and support you throughout the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What training methods do you use?

I use reward-based, relationship-focused training methods rooted in positive reinforcement and modern behavioral science.

I do not use or recommend methods that rely on fear, pain, intimidation, or unnecessary discomfort.

This includes shock collars, prong collars, and choke chains.

My goal is to help you teach and guide your dog through clear communication, management, positive reinforcement, and practical training strategies.

Do you offer virtual training?

Yes. I offer one-on-one virtual dog training throughout the United States.

Virtual training allows your dog to learn in their familiar environment while giving you the skills and confidence to work directly with your dog.

What do I need for a virtual training session?
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Phone, tablet, or computer with camera and microphone
  • Quiet training space
  • Your dog
  • Treats, toys, or training supplies
What types of training can you help with?
  • Puppy foundations
  • Basic manners
  • Jumping
  • Recall foundations
  • Loose leash walking
  • Focus and engagement
  • Household manners
  • Confidence building
  • Enrichment
  • Impulse control
Do you work with aggression or serious behavior concerns?

Some behavior concerns require highly specialized experience and support.

At this time, I may not be the best fit for cases involving severe aggression, significant bite history, or other serious safety concerns.

When appropriate, I may recommend consultation with a qualified behavior professional.

How do I know which service is right for me?

If you're unsure where to start, schedule a Virtual Training Consultation. We'll discuss your dog, your goals, and determine the best next steps together.

Booking & Payment Policies

Exceptions may be made for emergencies at my discretion.

Missed Appointments

Missed appointments without notice may be considered used and are generally non-refundable.

If you experience technical difficulties, please contact me as soon as possible.

Important Training Information

Can you guarantee results?

No trainer can ethically guarantee results.

Progress depends on many factors including consistency, practice, environment, management, and your dog's individual needs.

Will I receive homework?

Yes.

Practice between sessions is an important part of training success. You'll receive personalized recommendations and exercises to work on.

What if my dog doesn't cooperate?

That's completely okay.

Dogs have challenging days just like people do. Training should never be about forcing performance. We'll adjust as needed and support your dog where they are.

Health & Safety

Health Considerations

Please let me know about any medical conditions, injuries, medications, or other concerns that may affect training.

If pain or a medical condition may be contributing to behavior, I may recommend consulting your veterinarian.

Training Equipment & Methods

Equipment Policy

I use reward-based, relationship-focused training methods.

I do not use or recommend aversive tools such as:

  • Prong collars
  • Choke chains
  • Electronic collars

However, this is a judgment-free space.

Many dog guardians were introduced to these tools by trainers, friends, family members, or other sources. My goal is never to shame or criticize where someone is starting.

Instead, I work with clients to identify practical, humane alternatives that support both learning and well-being.

Client Responsibilities

Your Role in Training
  • Practice recommendations between sessions
  • Provide a safe environment for your dog
  • Supervise appropriately
  • Communicate important behavior changes
  • Provide accurate information about your dog's history

Dog training is a collaborative process, and success depends on everyone working together.
