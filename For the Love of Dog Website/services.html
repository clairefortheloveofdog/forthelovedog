Why Positive Reinforcement?

At For the Love of Dog, I use force-free, reward-based methods built on modern learning science.

Training should never rely on fear, pain, intimidation, or punishment.

Instead, we focus on teaching dogs what to do, understanding their communication, and creating learning experiences where dogs can succeed.

Understand the dog. Change the behavior. Build the relationship.
